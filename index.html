Game Guide

How to Play NEXUS

A fast-paced test of numerical sequence tracing and route planning.

1

Start at [1]

Touch or click on node [1] to initiate the path. On reverse levels, start at [25].

2

Follow Sequence

Drag through adjacent unnumbered sockets to reach milestone checkpoints in numerical order.

3

Drag Back to Undo

Hit a dead end? Drag backward along your line to undo any step without resetting the timer.

4

Beat the Clock

Connect all 25 nodes before the 20-second timer expires to advance to progressive streak levels.
